VideoGram

Automated short-form video creation

PythonLLMText-to-speechImage generationffmpeg
Personal project

Turns a written script into a finished video or reel. VideoGram connects an LLM, a voice generator, and an image model, then assembles the final video with ffmpeg. A scene-approval step keeps a human in control before anything is rendered.

Inside the project

The problem

Making short-form video by hand means recording voiceover, planning shots, sourcing visuals, and editing everything together. VideoGram automates the repetitive parts and leaves the creative decisions to the creator.

How it works

You write the script and hand it over. VideoGram generates the voiceover, builds a scene and shot plan, and generates an image for each shot.

Human in the loop

You review and approve the scenes before rendering, so weak shots get caught early instead of after the video is built. ffmpeg then combines the voiceover and approved visuals into the final video.
